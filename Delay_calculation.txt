*=========================================================
* Delay Calculation
*=========================================================

* Output a0
.meas time1 FIND time WHEN V(a0)=1 RISE=1
.meas time2 FIND time WHEN V(b0)=1 FALL=1
.meas TPHL PARAM time2-time1

* Output a1
.meas time3 FIND time WHEN V(a1)=1 FALL=1
.meas time4 FIND time WHEN V(b1)=1 RISE=1
.meas TPLH PARAM time4-time3

* Average Propagation Delay
.meas delay PARAM (TPHL+TPLH)/2


*=========================================================
* Fall Time
*=========================================================

.meas timea FIND time WHEN V(a0)=1 RISE=1
.meas timeb FIND time WHEN V(b0)=1 FALL=1
.meas tfall PARAM timeb-timea


*=========================================================
* Rise Time
*=========================================================

.meas timec FIND time WHEN V(a1)=1 FALL=1
.meas timed FIND time WHEN V(b1)=1 RISE=1
.meas trise PARAM timed-timec

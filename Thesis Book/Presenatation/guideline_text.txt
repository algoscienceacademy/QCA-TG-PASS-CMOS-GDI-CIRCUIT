Welcome to the Final Thesis/Project
Presentation
• Purpose: To ensure clarity,
professionalism, and smooth execution of
the presentations.
• Reminder: This presentation covers the
essential guidelines and expectations for
the day.

Be On Time
• • Arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled
presentation time.
• • Late arrivals may result in disqualification
or penalties.
• • Ensure all setup (hardware, slides, etc.)
is ready before your turn.

Timing Breakdown
• • Presentation Time: 10 minutes
• • Question & Answer Session: 5 minutes
• • Total: 15 minutes per student/project
group.
• • Exceeding the time limit may affect your
marks.

Show Your Work!
• • Hardware demonstration is mandatory
where applicable.
• • Prepare your hardware and verify
functionality before your presentation.
• • Make sure all connections are secure
and equipment is ready to avoid delays.

Crafting Your Presentation

• Avoid lengthy paragraphs; use short, bullet-point text.

• Use professional slide templates.
• Ensure text is legible (minimum font size: 20).

Professional Appearance
• • Male Students: Decent outfit.
• • Female Students:
• - Modest formal attire with proper hijab
• - Avoid bright or flashy colors.
• • Overall: Ensure neat and tidy presentation.

Before Your Presentation
• • Bring all required materials (hardware,
laptop, pen drive, etc.).
• • Double-check your slides for errors and
flow and submit to the given link
beforehand.
• • Practice timing your presentation to fit
within 10 minutes.
• • Inform the committee of any technical
requirements in advance.

How You’ll Be Assessed
• • Content Quality: Depth, accuracy, and
relevance
• • Presentation Skills: Clarity, confidence,
and engagement
• • Q&A Performance: Ability to answer
questions effectively
• • Hardware Demonstration: Functionality
and innovation

Need Help?
• • Contact your supervisor for guidance on
corrections.
• • Reach out to the Thesis/Project
Committee for any clarification.
